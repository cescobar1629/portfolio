TouchDesigner Projects

I'm a student at Stony Brook University building real-time particle systems in TouchDesigner. My work draws on depth cameras, LiDAR, and Arduino-based sensors to translate physical space and movement into responsive light, projected back into the room through custom projection-mapping setups.

Production: Coriolanus

Coriolanus (Fall 2024)

Mark Morris Dance Center / James and Martha Duffy Performance Space / Brooklyn, NY

Site-specific real-time scenography engineered for the production of Coriolanus in Fall 2024. Dynamic particle wave velocity vectors and recursive GLSL feedback were projected across the Duffy cyclorama wall, reacting dynamically to the performer's kinetic pacing, shadow silhouette, and ground contact.
